Troubleshooting & Tips

If you encounter any issues logging in, follow these quick steps in any web browser:

  1. Sign in to PlayStation: Go to playstation.com and sign in with your account.
  2. Get your code: Once signed in, open this link:
    https://ca.account.sony.com/api/v1/ssocookie
  3. Copy and Paste: Copy what appears on that page (the 64-character code or the whole line) and paste it into Step 3.
