AstraPlay is a high-performance, open-source Remote Play and Cloud Streaming client for PS4 and PS5 consoles, designed for mobile, tablet, and Android TV devices.

KEY FEATURES:
• High Performance Streaming: Up to 1080p 60 FPS (PS4/PS5) and 4K (PSCloud) with low-latency hardware decoding (H.264 & HEVC/H.265).
• Cloud Streaming Support: Stream games from your library or catalog directly without leaving your couch.
• Automatic Discovery: Effortlessly discover and wake up registered consoles across your local network.
• Mobile & Touch Friendly: Customizable on-screen touch controls, virtual touchpad, and gyroscope/motion sensor support.
• Full Controller Support: Compatible with Bluetooth controllers, USB gamepads, and DualSense features including rumble and haptics.
• Android TV Optimized: Full DPAD navigation, leanback launcher support, and TV-friendly interface.

SETUP REQUIREMENTS:
1. A PS4 or PS5 console on your network (or supported cloud streaming account).
2. For remote play over the internet, port forwarding or a VPN to your home network is recommended.
